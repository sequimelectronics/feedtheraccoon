# About

Becky R. Northaven is an independent AI-safety researcher in Sequim, Washington. Her day job is owning [Sequim Electronics](https://sequimelectronics.com), a computer and electronics shop.

She works independently, with a team of AI agents she directs and reviews. Her preprint, *No Step Looked Wrong* (2026), is on Zenodo and alphaXiv, and a short version was submitted to an AAAI-27 workshop. She has also released Debate Hall, an open tool for structured paper review. Right now she is testing larger models, writing scenarios based on classic literature, and building a conversation library for safety classifiers.

She builds small, local, private AI. She has been a D&D player all her life.

The message is better testing, not less AI.
